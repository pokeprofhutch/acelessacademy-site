Powerful Hand Alakazam Deck Guide

by Rubis

Overview

Powerful Hand Alakazam is a Psychic-type deck built around maximizing your hand size to power up Alakazam's Powerful Hand attack. The deck focuses on quickly evolving into Alakazam while using multiple support Pokémon and Trainer cards to build resources and maintain a large hand.

Game Plan

Your ideal start is going first and setting up for a Turn 2 Rare Candy into Alakazam. Use cards like Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Dudunsparce, and Telepathic Energy to quickly establish your board while keeping your hand full.

Telepathic Energy provides additional search utility by helping find Basic Pokémon when needed, while Abra's Teleporter Ability can help move an early Abra out of the Active Spot and improve your setup.

Once Alakazam is in play, focus on maintaining a large hand size to maximize Powerful Hand's damage output. Support Pokémon like Dedenne help recover important Trainer cards, while Elgyem provides disruption by moving key Energy away from opposing attackers. Shaymin protects vulnerable Basic Pokémon on your Bench from being easily Knocked Out.

Strengths

  • Can create explosive turns with a fast Turn 2 Alakazam.
  • Powerful Hand scales with your hand size, rewarding strong resource management.
  • Multiple support Pokémon provide consistency, recovery, and disruption.
  • Flexible disruption options against Energy-based strategies.

Weaknesses

  • Requires careful resource management to maintain a large hand.
  • Depends on evolving into Alakazam quickly.
  • Losing key support Pokémon can slow the deck down.
  • Early pressure from aggressive decks can prevent setup.

Final Thoughts

Powerful Hand Alakazam rewards players who enjoy planning ahead and managing resources. With the right setup, it can turn a large hand into massive damage while using clever support Pokémon to disrupt opponents and maintain control of the game.
